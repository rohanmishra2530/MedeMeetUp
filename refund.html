1. Cancellation Policy

At MedeMeetUp, we understand that medical professional schedules can be unpredictable. However, to ensure seminars remain viable for organizers and other participants, we maintain a strict cancellation window.

The 48-Hour Rule:

Cancellations must be made at least 48 hours prior to the scheduled start time of the seminar to be eligible for a refund consideration. This allows us to offer the vacant seat to other students on the waitlist.

  • Cancellations made more than 48 hours in advance: Eligible for a full refund of the seminar fee (minus processing fees).
  • Cancellations made less than 48 hours in advance: Non-refundable, as organizers have already committed resources based on registration numbers.

2. Refund Eligibility

Refunds are typically granted in the following specific circumstances:

Seminar Cancellation

If the organizer cancels the seminar for any reason, all registered attendees will receive a 100% refund automatically.

Technical Failures

For digital seminars, if MedeMeetUp experiences a platform-wide outage preventing access, refunds will be issued upon verification.

Rescheduling

If a seminar is moved to a new date/time that the user cannot attend, a refund request may be submitted within 24 hours of the change notice.

Duplicate Billing

In the event of accidental duplicate registration for the same session, the extra payment will be fully refunded.

3. Refund Process

Once a refund is approved, we initiate the transfer immediately. The timeline for the funds appearing in your account depends on your financial institution:

1

Submission

Submit your request via the "My Bookings" section or by contacting support.

2

Review (24-48 hours)

Our billing team reviews the request against the cancellation policy and eligibility criteria.

3

Processing (5-7 Business Days)

Funds are sent back to the original payment method. Please note that credit card providers may take up to a full billing cycle to display the credit.

4. Non-Refundable Items

The following charges are strictly non-refundable to cover administrative and platform operational costs:

  • close Platform Service Fees (typically 3-5%)
  • close Late cancellations (under 48 hours)
  • close Non-attendance (No-shows)
  • close Promotional or Discounted "Final Sale" tickets
  • close Third-party materials purchased through seminars
  • close Certificate issuance fees (if already processed)
